frame 02 · selected work

Wheels Up — an aviation analytics design system

Private aviation · internal BI ecosystem · design system · Exult Global

The problem wasn't a lack of dashboards. It was that every team read the same information differently — 60+ dashboards across operations, sales, finance, maintenance and leadership, with duplicated KPIs, inconsistent layouts, and dense, table-heavy reporting.

Partnering directly with the VP of Business Intelligence, I built an internal Figma reporting design system — KPI modules, chart structures, spacing and comparison rules — and standardized dashboards across departments so a metric meant the same thing everywhere.

  • 60+ dashboards unified
  • 40% usability improvement
  • 25% faster report generation

frame 06 · how I work

Designing for decisions, not just screens

The through-line across dashboards, HR systems, shipment tracking and deal workflows is the same: help people track, interpret, locate and act on business information without unnecessary friction. Complexity → structure → clarity → a product people actually adopt.

Tools: Figma, Framer, Adobe XD, Protopie, FigJam, Zeplin, HTML/CSS · plus an emerging practice in AI-assisted design and prototyping.
